# Madras Magic — Taste Trend Tracking

This file tracks **hypotheses** about how the client's taste may be evolving over time, based on
the sequence and recency of approvals/rejections — not settled rules. Treat everything here as
"worth watching," not "known fact." A hypothesis only graduates to a confirmed rule in
`client_profile.md` once there's enough consistent evidence and Vengatesh agrees it reflects a
real shift, not a coincidence.

## How to use this file

- **When syncing new data**: check whether new approvals/rejections support, weaken, or contradict
  an existing hypothesis below. Update the evidence count and date range accordingly.
- **When generating a new design**: you may mention an active hypothesis as a soft signal (e.g.
  "recent approvals lean toward X, worth considering") but never present it as a confirmed
  preference the way hard rules in `client_profile.md` are presented.
- **When a hypothesis gets enough evidence** (a reasonable bar: 4+ consistent examples with no
  contradicting cases, spanning more than one project) — propose to Vengatesh that it graduate to
  a confirmed rule in `client_profile.md`, and ask for confirmation before making that change.
- **Always state the evidence count explicitly** when referencing a hypothesis, e.g. "this is a
  hypothesis based on 2 examples so far, not yet a confirmed pattern."

## Important framing reminder

This file exists to make Vengatesh's (and Claude's) reasoning about *future* taste **transparent
and falsifiable** — every hypothesis here should be checkable against new evidence, not vibes.
Resist the temptation to declare a trend confirmed from a single example, even a strongly approved
one. One success or failure is an anecdote; a pattern needs repetition across more than one
project, ideally with no contradicting cases in between.

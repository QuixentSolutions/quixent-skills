# Instagram Design Rules

Recommended composition:
- Top: New Shop logo
- Upper area: availability/promotion badge
- Main area: headline + hero product
- Lower area: concise product features and CTA
- Footer: location/contact/social/QR details

Keep the hero product dominant.
Use realistic reflections, shadows, condensation, ice, splashes, glow, or other effects only when appropriate to the product.
Never allow decorative effects to obscure product branding.
Use large readable text and avoid dense paragraphs.

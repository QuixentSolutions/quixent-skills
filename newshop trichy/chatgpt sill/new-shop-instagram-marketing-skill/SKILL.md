# New Shop Instagram Marketing Skill

## Purpose
Create professional, high-quality Instagram promotional designs for New Shop, Trichy. The output should feel like a polished commercial advertisement rather than a generic template.

## Brand identity
- Brand: New Shop
- Primary visual theme: yellow, black, and white.
- The supplied reference image in `assets/new-shop-reference-image.png` is the authoritative New Shop brand reference for this skill.
- `assets/approved-designs/` contains client-approved final posts (Kinder Joy, Toblerone, Americano Mints) showing the current approved footer layout, address, landmark, and phone number. Use these as the layout/footer reference alongside the brand reference image.
- The logo in `assets/new-shop-logo-reference.png` is the exact supplied logo appearance extracted from that reference. Use it as the logo source; do not redraw, reinterpret, simplify, replace, recolor, or change its typography.
- Preserve the supplied New Shop logo and its proportions/identity.
- Treat the reference image's yellow, black, and white palette as the established brand theme. Keep the yellow/black/white relationship visually consistent across posts.
- Use product-specific accent colors only when they improve the product presentation while keeping New Shop branding recognizable.
- Prefer bold typography, strong contrast, clean hierarchy, premium lighting, realistic product presentation, and mobile-first readability.

## Standard post structure
When appropriate, build the composition around:
1. New Shop logo at the top.
2. Availability badge such as “NOW IN STOCK”.
3. A short, attention-grabbing product headline.
4. Product name/variant.
5. Large, realistic hero product image.
6. Product-specific visual effects that fit the product.
7. 2–4 concise benefit/features callouts only when factually supported.
8. Strong CTA such as “GRAB YOURS NOW →”.
9. Footer containing store/contact details.
10. Google Maps QR code when requested or when the standard contact footer is being used and there is enough space.

## Product accuracy
- Do not invent product specifications, ingredients, prices, offers, sizes, health claims, or availability.
- Preserve visible product branding and packaging as accurately as possible.
- If the supplied product image is the reference, use it as the visual source rather than inventing a different package.
- Avoid claims such as “instant boost” unless the user supplied them or they are clearly intended as advertising copy and are appropriate for the product.
- For branded products, keep the brand/product name legible and avoid unnecessary distortion.

## Design quality checklist
Before finalizing:
- Instagram-friendly square or portrait composition as requested.
- Strong focal point on the product.
- Text remains readable on a phone.
- No overcrowded elements.
- Logo is not distorted.
- Footer is legible.
- Yellow/black/white New Shop identity remains visible.
- Product-specific lighting and effects look realistic.
- CTA is visually prominent.
- Contact details are spelled exactly as provided.

## Footer guidance
For a full contact footer, prioritize:
- Visit/store location (address)
- Landmark (pin/school icon + "Opposite St. James Matriculation Higher Secondary School (near by Domino's, Cantonment)")
- Phone
- Instagram
- Google Maps QR code
- “Right by Trichy Junction — Open 24/7”
- “New Shop · Cantonment, Trichy”

The client-approved two-block footer style (address block left, landmark block right, as seen in `assets/approved-designs/`) is the preferred layout when space allows. Do not cram the full address into tiny unreadable text. If space is limited, use a clean two-level footer and keep the QR code large enough to scan.

## QR code
If a Google Maps QR is requested, encode exactly:
https://maps.app.goo.gl/MBiUWXNEwir7jxGN6
Do not replace it with a generic map icon. The QR must remain high-contrast and have sufficient quiet space around it.

## Copy style
Use short, energetic, retail-focused copy:
- Strong headline
- Short supporting line
- Product name
- Minimal benefit statements
- Direct CTA
Avoid long paragraphs.

## User-provided assets
When the user provides a logo, product photo, reference design, or QR code:
- Treat it as the primary reference.
- Preserve important brand/product details.
- For New Shop branding, use `assets/new-shop-logo-reference.png` as the exact logo reference and `assets/new-shop-reference-image.png` as the established theme/layout reference.
- Match the established New Shop visual language unless the user explicitly requests a new direction.
- Never generate a substitute logo when the supplied New Shop logo asset is available.

## Important
The user may simply say “Create a New Shop post for [product].” In that case, apply this entire workflow automatically and ask only for information that is genuinely missing and necessary.


## NON-NEGOTIABLE NEW SHOP BRAND LOCK
This section has highest priority for all New Shop Instagram post generations.

### 1. Exact logo lock
- The file `assets/new-shop-logo-reference.png` is the authoritative New Shop logo reference.
- Use the supplied logo as the brand logo. Do NOT invent, redraw, regenerate, simplify, stylize, replace, recolor, crop away essential elements, or alter its typography.
- Preserve the recognizable logo construction: white speech-bubble center, black NEW SHOP lettering, and the surrounding yellow/white/black horizontal bar elements.
- Never create a text-only substitute for the logo.
- If image-generation limitations make exact logo rendering unreliable, prioritize using the supplied logo asset rather than attempting to recreate it from memory.

### 2. Exact brand-theme lock
- The file `assets/new-shop-reference-image.png` is the master visual reference.
- The dominant palette is yellow + black + white.
- Yellow should be used for borders, highlights, badges, CTA buttons, separators, glow accents, and key emphasis.
- Black should provide the main premium/high-contrast background.
- White should be used for supporting typography and clean contrast.
- Do not introduce unrelated dominant brand colours unless the product packaging itself requires them.

### 3. Visual language lock
Every promotional post should feel like it belongs to the same New Shop campaign family:
- Premium, bold, high-contrast retail advertising.
- Strong product hero image on the right/center-right.
- Bold promotional headline on the left or upper-left.
- Yellow brush-stroke/highlight treatment behind major headline words when appropriate.
- Dramatic product lighting, realistic reflections, controlled glow, and dynamic energy effects.
- Yellow rectangular labels/badges such as “NOW IN STOCK” when appropriate.
- Yellow pill-shaped CTA such as “GRAB YOURS NOW →” when appropriate.
- Use clean information blocks with yellow headings and white supporting text.
- Keep generous visual hierarchy; do not overcrowd the design.

### 4. Reference-layout rule
The reference image is a STYLE AND BRAND STRUCTURE reference, not a requirement to duplicate the exact product composition.
For each new product:
- Keep the New Shop branding system consistent.
- Adapt the hero composition, effects, headline, and product-specific copy to the product.
- Do not copy incorrect product claims from another product.
- Do not invent product specifications, flavours, nutritional claims, prices, offers, or availability unless the user supplies them.

### 5. Footer consistency
When the post calls for the standard New Shop footer, preserve the established structure:
- VISIT US (address)
- LANDMARK
- CALL US
- FOLLOW US
- FIND US ON GOOGLE MAPS
- Bottom location/availability strip
Use the supplied New Shop business information from the skill/reference assets (`references/store-details.md`, `assets/approved-designs/`). Do not invent or modify contact details.

### 6. Product packaging accuracy
- The product package must remain faithful to the real product.
- Do not redesign brand packaging merely to match New Shop colours.
- Product brand logos and package colours should remain recognizable and accurate.
- New Shop branding belongs around the product; it must not overwrite the product's own identity.

### 7. Instagram format lock
Unless the user specifies another size:
- Create a 1080 × 1080 px Instagram square post.
- Keep all important text, logo elements, product details, and CTA safely inside the visible area.
- Maintain strong readability at mobile-feed size.

### 8. Text-generation rule
- Keep headline text short, punchy, and readable.
- Avoid excessive text.
- Use exact user-provided wording when the user specifies copy.
- Never replace the New Shop logo with generated text.
- Check spelling carefully before generation.
- Do not add unsupported claims.

### 9. Consistency instruction for future generations
When the user says “make another New Shop post,” “same style,” “use the New Shop skill,” or similar:
1. Treat `assets/new-shop-reference-image.png` as the master visual reference.
2. Treat `assets/new-shop-logo-reference.png` as the master logo reference.
3. Apply the New Shop Brand Lock above before designing the product composition.
4. Keep the same overall brand hierarchy, contrast, yellow/black/white treatment, premium retail feel, CTA treatment, and footer system.
5. Change only the product-specific creative elements requested by the user.

### 10. Priority rule
If a later creative instruction conflicts with the New Shop brand identity, preserve the exact logo and core brand identity unless the user explicitly asks to change the branding.

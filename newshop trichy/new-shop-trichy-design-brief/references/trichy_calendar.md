**Maintenance note:** Tamil festival dates follow a lunar/solar mix and shift yearly. Dates below are for **2026** — re-verify before reusing in 2027+. Where a source didn't give an exact 2026 date, it's marked "verify closer to date."

## Trichy-specific / non-religious angles (year-round, not date-locked)

These matter more for this particular outlet's location than most religious festivals:

- **College reopening / exam seasons** (June and December-ish) — even though this outlet isn't college-belt, exam-season snacking/late-night study angles can work city-wide on Meta if targeting isn't hyper-local
- **Monsoon season** (Oct–Dec, Northeast monsoon in Tamil Nadu) — hot beverages, umbrellas/rain-day convenience angle
- **Summer** (Apr–Jun) — cold beverages/coolers, matches New Shop's own past "Beat the Heat" summer cooler campaigns seen nationally
- **Railway/travel peak periods** (long weekends, festival travel rush at Trichy Junction) — "grab it before you board" angle, since the store is walking distance from the junction
- **Hospital-visitor moments** (year-round, not seasonal) — quick-need angle: medicines, snacks, phone recharge for people visiting Joseph Eye Hospital, KMC Speciality, or the Govt Medical College nearby

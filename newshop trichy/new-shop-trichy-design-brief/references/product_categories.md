## ⚠️ Ad-compliance flag: smoking accessories

The list includes lighters, rolling papers, and hookah coal (Stash Pro brand). **Meta's advertising policies restrict or prohibit promoting smoking accessories and drug paraphernalia** — this category should not be featured in Meta ad creative even if it's genuinely stocked in-store. Safe to mention in-store signage or organic (non-paid) content only if locally legal and appropriate; flag to Vengatesh before including in any paid campaign.

## What this means for the skill

- Hooks/campaigns can now safely reference real categories (snacks, chocolates, soft drinks, energy drinks, ice cream, juices, protein bars) instead of a generic "everything in one stop" line.
- Festival-tied campaigns (Diwali, Vinayagar Chaturthi, etc.) can now name real products — e.g. chocolates/sweets for Diwali gifting, energy drinks/snacks for exam season, ice cream for summer — instead of vague placeholders.
- Don't feature Stash Pro / smoking accessories in any Meta ad creative (see flag above).

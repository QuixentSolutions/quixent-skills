# Audience Profile — New Shop, Bharathiar Salai, Cantonment, Trichy

Based on confirmed location anchors (see SKILL.md). This outlet's natural audience is different from a college-belt or temple-town New Shop location.

## Primary segments

1. **Transit commuters** — Trichy Junction railway station (~0.8km) and Central Bus Stand (~0.5km) nearby. Need: grab-and-go snacks/beverages, quick essentials before travel, phone recharge, printing/ticket-booking services if offered.
2. **Hospital visitors/staff** — Joseph Eye Hospital, KMC Speciality, K.A.P. Viswanathan Govt Medical College all nearby. Need: OTC medicines, quick snacks/beverages during long hospital waits, odd-hour convenience (fits New Shop's 24/7 positioning well).
3. **Cantonment commercial-area shoppers** — general foot traffic from the Bharathiar Salai/Williams Road commercial belt (shops like Nalli Silks nearby). Need: everyday FMCG, quick errands.

## Segments this outlet is NOT primarily serving

- College students (no major college directly adjacent to this address — don't default to youth/campus-life creative)
- Temple pilgrims (Srirangam/Rockfort are elsewhere in Trichy, not walking distance from this address) — unless a specific travel-through angle is being tested

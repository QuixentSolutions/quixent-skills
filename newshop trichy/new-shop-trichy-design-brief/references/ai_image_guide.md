# AI Image Generation Guide — New Shop Trichy

Unlike other clients in this workspace, AI-generated imagery is allowed and expected here. But "allowed" isn't "no standard" — use this checklist.

## When AI-generated is fine

- Lifestyle/mood imagery (e.g., "commuter grabbing a coffee before boarding a train")
- Festival-themed decorative visuals (diyas, kolam patterns, festive backgrounds)
- Product-adjacent illustrative shots when no real product photo exists yet
- Seasonal backdrop imagery (monsoon, summer)

## When a real photo is required instead

- **The actual storefront/signage** — never AI-generate this; people need to recognize the real location near Trichy Junction/Central Bus Stand when searching "near me"
- Any claim about a specific product New Shop actually stocks (don't show a product that isn't real in a way that implies it's sold here)
- Any offer/price callout — pair AI background art with real, accurate pricing/text, not AI-hallucinated numbers

## Quality checklist before sending

- [ ] No garbled or nonsensical text rendered inside the image (classic AI tell) — if the creative needs text, overlay real typography in post, don't rely on the model to render it
- [ ] Hands, packaging, and product shapes look physically plausible — check closely, this is the most common AI-image giveaway
- [ ] Color palette doesn't clash with what's known of brand guidelines (see `brand_guidelines.md`) once locked colors are confirmed
- [ ] Setting/context looks like Trichy/Tamil Nadu, not generic-Western or unmistakably-another-region (clothing, signage language, street style)
- [ ] Doesn't imply a product or service this outlet doesn't actually offer (check against confirmed product mix once available)

## Style consistency

Once more campaigns run, log recurring successful prompt patterns here (lighting style, color grading, composition) so future generations stay visually consistent across a campaign rather than looking like disconnected one-offs.

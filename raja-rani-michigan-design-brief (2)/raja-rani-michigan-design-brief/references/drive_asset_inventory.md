## Poster types observed (by category)

**Weekend Special (multi-dish menu boards)** — the most common format, used almost every Friday/weekend:
- IMG-...WA0000, WA0011, WA0016, WA0020, WA0026, WA0027, WA0029, WA0036, WA0044, WA0043, WA0053, WA0062
- Pattern: black background, gold "WEEKEND SPECIAL" headline under the logo, 4–9 dish names with prices stacked in gold/white text (sometimes split Veg / Non-Veg columns), one hero food photo (sometimes 2–3 smaller ones), HALAL badge, "ORDER NOW" gold CTA badge, standard footer.

**Single hero dish spotlight** — one dish, dramatic food photography, short punchy tagline:
- WA0007 (Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani), WA0009, WA0010 (Chicken Vindaloo), WA0012 (Shrimp Curry), WA0015 (Boti Curry), WA0021 (Nalli Gosht Biryani), WA0033 (Fish Pakoda), WA0037 (free biryani stamp-card promo art), WA0042 (Kulfi), WA0048 (Paneer Tikka), WA0049 (Karampodi Shrimp), WA0052 (Chilli Paneer)
- Pattern: full-bleed or large food photo, 3–6 word punchy tagline in gold script/display type, logo top, minimal text, HALAL badge, footer.
- **Dark-brown/bronze background variant** (newly confirmed — "100% Khoa Raja Rani Kulfi"): instead of black or yellow, a warm dark-brown/bronze backdrop (evokes a mithai shop / antique brass table setting) with an ornate gold flourish under the logo, a "HOME MADE" + flavor-count ribbon badge, and a round "NATURAL 100%" leaf badge used in place of (or alongside) the Halal badge for dessert/sweet items. Use this warmer palette specifically for kulfi/mithai/dessert spotlights where a cooler black or bright yellow bg would feel off-tone.
- **Decorative silhouette backdrop** (newly confirmed — Fish Pakoda poster): a black pagoda/tree silhouette illustration sits in the corner of a yellow-background spotlight as a textural accent, alongside a handwritten "MUST TRY" callout and doodled stars/motion-lines. Small hand-drawn doodle accents (arrows, stars, "must try" scrawl) are an approved lightweight embellishment for hero-dish posters, used sparingly.
- **Playful/pun tagline variant** (newly confirmed — "Bowl of Magic Noodles" for Hakka Noodles): script + bold-caps two-line headline with a playful pun/wordplay rather than a straight adjective tagline ("Crispy. Spicy. Addictive." style). Both straight-adjective and playful-pun taglines are acceptable; pick based on how fun vs. premium the dish framing should feel.

**Combo / bundle / limited-time offer:**
- WA0018 (Holiday Biryani Combo pricing table), WA0039 (Spring & Summer Combo), WA0040 (Snacks/Drinks/Main Course mini-menu card)
- Pattern: itemized combo pricing in a clean table/list, "LIMITED-TIME" or date-bound callout, gold borders.
- **Marquee-light badge variant** (newly confirmed — "Holiday Special Biryani Combo"): the offer headline sits inside a rounded rectangle bordered with small gold marquee/bulb dots (like a theater marquee), used for a festive combo announcement; combo tiers are priced per protein (Veg/Egg/Paneer/Chicken/Goat) rather than per fixed bundle, and a "LIMITED-TIME HOLIDAY OFFER — UNTIL [date]" dual-badge stacks to the side. Good template for any dated, tiered-by-protein combo offer.

**Festival / seasonal / cultural event:**
- WA0024 (Diwali greeting), WA0035 (Tamil New Year & Ugadi — Banana Leaf Feast, event registration), WA0064 (Diwali Special Unlimited Buffet with date/time/pricing), WA0047 (Diwali menu + pricing), WA0038 (Valentine's Day discount), WA0032 (Women's Day), WA0022/WA0023 (New Year Special menu)
- Pattern: festive motif (diya/lights for Diwali, banana leaf spread for Tamil New Year) blended with the black/gold palette rather than replacing it, event date/time prominent, sometimes a registration/booking CTA instead of "Order Now."
- **New Year Specials — itemized dated menu** (newly confirmed): black starry-sky background, large 3D-gold "NEW YEAR SPECIALS" display lettering, a full itemized dish+price list (not just a teaser), a "Only on 31st Dec & 1st Jan" starburst date badge, and a premium ribbon-style "HALAL CERTIFIED" seal (rosette + ribbon tails) as an alternate Halal-badge treatment for extra-premium festival posters.
- **Valentine's Day — numeric gimmick confirmed**: the discount percentage is tied playfully to the date (14% off, for Feb 14th) inside a red heart-shaped color field with ribbon banners ("VALENTINE'S DAY SPECIAL" / "FOR BOTH DINE-IN & TO-GO" / "JUST ON FEBRUARY 14TH!"), small heart emoji/graphics scattered around. When a festival has a numeric date, consider echoing that number in the discount if it reads naturally (do not force it otherwise).
- **Women's Day — raffle/prize mechanic** (newly confirmed, distinct from a straight discount): "Get a chance to win Silver Coin" — a gift box + engraved silver coin in a velvet ring-box + a raffle-ticket drum, dated window ("MARCH 1 TO 8"), "Dine In & To Go" note. Confirms festival posters can carry a prize-drawing/raffle mechanic instead of (or alongside) a discount — pick whichever mechanic fits what the client is actually running that period.

**Recurring weekly promotions (always the same day):**
- WA0019 (Chinese Combo Night — Tuesday), WA0025/WA0031/WA0034 (Chat Night — Wednesday $5), WA0041 (Unlimited Dosa Buffet — Wednesday), WA0050 (Unlimited Dosa Night, 25+ varieties), WA0030 (Maggie/Hakka Noodles bowl)

- **Dosa Buffet variety count varies** — confirmed two separate Dosa Buffet posters advertise different variety counts (15 varieties in one, 25+ in another). Don't hardcode a specific number; always pull the current variety count from the client/menu at request time rather than reusing an old figure.
- **Chat Night — "content creator" photography variant** (newly confirmed): same Wednesday $5 chat mechanic, but the hero shot shows hands holding up smartphones actively photographing the chat spread on the table (candlelit, moody restaurant ambience) rather than just a plated food shot. This social-media-native framing is a valid alternative to the plain food-photo Chat Night layout — use when the brief wants a more "shareable/Instagrammable" feel.

**Weekend Special — grid/circular-photo variant:**
- Alongside the vertical-list Weekend Special format already logged, a second recurring layout uses a 3×3 (or 3×2) grid of circular/hexagonal dish photos bordering a central Veg/Non-Veg text block ("WEEKEND Special" as a large headline). Treat both the vertical-list-with-1-3-photos version and this photo-grid version as valid layouts — pick whichever suits the number of dishes (fewer dishes → vertical list with hero photos; many dishes → grid).
- **Seasonal decoration overlay confirmed**: the same grid layout has been reskinned with small winter/snowflake accents scattered in the black background corners for a winter-timed Weekend Special, and separately with a large single hero photo plus itemized dish list underneath (Kuzhi Paniyaram, Mini Idlis, etc.) for a South Indian breakfast/tiffin-themed Weekend Special. Confirms Weekend Special is a flexible framework — vertical list, grid, or list-over-one-big-photo — chosen by dish count and seasonal flavor, not a fixed template.
